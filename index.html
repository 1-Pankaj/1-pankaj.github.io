06 / Questions

Common questions

Who do you build apps and websites for?

For founders, teams and companies, and for classrooms. I have also built products for my own studio, Codeloom.

Can I hire you as a freelance developer?

Yes. I take on new projects for clients worldwide as an independent developer. Email me your goal and timeline and I’ll reply with the best way to get there.

Do you build for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. I build iPhone and Android apps, and some projects are Android only.

Will you publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. Publishing on the stores is part of the last step, along with paid plans and fixes after release.

Which tools do you use?

For apps, mostly React Native and Kotlin. For websites, Next.js and Node.js. Firebase stores app data and RevenueCat handles paid subscriptions.

How long will my project take?

It depends on your goal and your deadline. The first step is a short call to work that out together.

Where are you based, and how do we start?

I’m in Jodhpur, India, and I work with clients worldwide. Email me your goal and timeline and I’ll reply with the best way to get there.
